Cover the back camera with your fingertip, then tap Start. Stay still for 20 seconds.

For a good reading

  1. Rest your index fingertip gently over the main back lens. Pressing hard blocks blood flow.
  2. Sit near a bright lamp or window. Light shining through your finger is what the camera reads.
  3. Keep your hand and phone still until the bar fills.

Safari can't switch on the iPhone flash from a web page, so bright room light matters. This is an estimate for curiosity and fitness, not a medical device.
